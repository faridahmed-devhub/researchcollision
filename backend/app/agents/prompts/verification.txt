STATUS RULES
VERIFIED   — claim is directly supported by stored paper text (quote matches).
INFERRED   — claim follows reasonably from stored evidence but adds interpretation.
SPECULATIVE— plausible but weakly supported connection.
UNKNOWN    — no supporting evidence found.
UNVERIFIED — citation failed validation against the paper store.

Never upgrade a status without matching stored evidence. Never delete
evidence; only downgrade or annotate.
