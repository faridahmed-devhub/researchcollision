### 4.1 CRITICAL — `embedding` system is misdescribed

The Subject paper's Abstract, §2, §3.2, and Appendix A say the `embedding` system performed
**Sentence-BERT dense retrieval** and cite Reimers 2019 / Karpukhin 2020 / sbert for it
(paper.md:77, 101; appendix_a:8; Abstract "Neural retrieval" §2). The evaluation's `embedding`
baseline **ran MockEmbeddingProvider** in all cases; the 36 embedding EV records were scored by
cosine similarity over **deterministic mock vectors**, not any embedding checkpoint. All relevant
sources agree (`baselines.py` author docstring, run-recorded config, README, UPGRADE_PLAN).
The paper therefore **misrepresents its second comparison system**, and the BERT/DPR/sbert citations
are unsupportive of the actual pipeline behavior. Title-level claim "evaluates sentence-embedding
retrieval" is not what was run.

### 4.2 HIGH — `llm_only`/`pipeline` "real Ollama" is supported by artifacts but contradicted by repo docs; not traceable

- **Supported by:** recorded config `llm_provider: openai_compatible` (results.json:141), real
  runtime errors (`ReadTimeout`/`ReadError`/`ConnectError` in `failures[]`), and free-form,
  non-template llm_only prose.
- **Contradicted by:** `report.md` line "Language-model steps used the deterministic mock LLM
  provider (no model API credentials were configured for this run)" (report.py:317) and README
  "v1 pilot used the mock LLM / mock embeddings providers" (README:86-87).
- That `report.md` disclaimer is a **static canned sentence** appended by `report.py` regardless of
  the actual provider; it was never corrected for the real run.
- **Not traceable:** no raw LLM call traces are persisted; endpoint/model/API key live in `.env`
  (not in repo). The paper's Abstract "real Ollama (remote, OpenAI-compatible)" (extract:92) vs
  §3.2/"self-hosted Ollama endpoint" (paper.md §11) are two contradicting phrasings.
  Recommend a single, provable phrasing ("openai_compatible endpoint; identity redacted").

### 4.3 HIGH — "one deterministic run, seed 0" is not fully supported

- `runner.py` accepts `--seed` with **default 0**; nothing in `results.json` stores the seed
  (0 occurrences of `"seed"` outside `blind_key` record content). Enabled: `paper.md:68, 107, 227,
  244` and Abstract; §8 says "deterministic run" and "seed 0".
- A real remote LLM at real temperature is not deterministically reproducible (REPRODUCIBILITY.md
  and caching-paper v2 both say the LLM sampling is not controlled); `seed` is "forwarded where the
  endpoint supports deterministic sampling" — i.e., **not guaranteed**. "Deterministic-seed (0)"
  is a strong word for what was actually a **seeded, single execution whose LLM sampling is
  non-reproducible**. Recommend "single seeded run; LLM sampling not guaranteed deterministic".

### 4.4 HIGH — 61 discarded analysis records are never disclosed

`failure_analysis.json` documents **61 records excluded or flagged** during evidence/quality
checking: by_type `ungrounded_hypothesis=18, gap_without_evidence=15,
intersection_without_evidence=23, system_error=4, no_expected_topic_covered=1`;
by_system `llm_only=53, pipeline=7, embedding=1`; by_stage `reasoning=19, retrieval=38,
generation=4`. The paper describes only the 4 runtime `failures` and never mentions this
`failure_analysis` layer — nor the "no_quality_claims" framing — so LLM-only-derived totals
(53) are presented without mentioning the 53 flagged llm_only records. The paper's transparency
box is therefore incomplete.

## 7. Render quality — literal markdown/HTML leaked into PDF (root cause identified)

The shipped PDF (the exact PDF under audit) **visibly contains** markdown/HTML fragments as text.
All are reproducible in `render_research_paper.py`; these are not OCR artifacts of extraction —
they are present in the PDF page text:

1. **Raw caption divs** — `<div class="table-caption">Table C/B/D: ...</div>` on pages 5, 6, 7;
   from `insert_captions()` (render:547-558) injecting an unstripped literal and being mis-parsed.
2. **ATX `##`/`#` headers** — `## A.1 Systems`, `## A.2 Corpus`, `## A.3 Execution`, `## D.1`,
   `## D.2`, and `# Appendix A/B/C/D` appear literally (pages 12-14) because the appendix-removal
   regex `^#.*\n?$` is applied without `MULTILINE`, stripping only the first line.
3. **Bold erroneously preserved as literal `**`** — "**no evidence-quality or relevance-quality
   claims**" appears literally on p.4; the two fragments were separate list/paragraph chunks, so
   `\*\*[^*]+\*\*` could not match across the line split — and single `*` is never handled at all
   (render:182 `_INLINE`), giving literal `*not*` on p.7.
4. TOC has **two "Appendix A" entries** ("Full 12×4 evidence matrix" and "Methods and
   Configuration"); actual appendix files are numbered A–D but appendix numbering skipped/duplicated
   (paper.md:310-322). Abbreviation: appendices are not uniquely labeled A–E.

These are bending-type (layout/rendering) defects, but because they place `#`/`**`/`<div>` text in
a "publication-ready PDF", they are **content-facing** and should be fixed before external release.
